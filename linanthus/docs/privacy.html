Visiting this website

This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, contact forms, or cookies set by Linanthus Labs. Fonts and artwork are served as part of the website. Our hosting provider may process technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the site.

When you contact us

Email links open your email application. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, organization, and project description. We use that information to respond, discuss potential work, and manage our business relationship.

Our email and hosting providers may process information to provide their services. We do not sell your inquiry information or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect our rights.

Retention and requests

We retain correspondence as needed for the inquiry, our working relationship, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you have shared by emailing linanthuslabs@gmail.com. We will review your request and explain any information we need to retain.

Confidential information

Please begin with a general description of your project. Avoid sending confidential, sensitive, or proprietary material until we have agreed on appropriate arrangements.
