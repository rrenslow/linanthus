# Linanthus Labs brand

## Visual direction

Dark by default: deep forest backgrounds, cream text, sage highlights, and muted lavender. The five-petal mark is paired with specific service descriptions and Linanthus flower artwork. There is no automatic switch to a light theme.

## Botanical artwork

A new composition was produced using the built-in image-generation tool. It is generated botanical editorial artwork rather than a documentary photograph. The flower form was informed by [Calflora's Linanthus parryae record](https://www.calflora.org/app/taxon?crn=4883) and reference observations, including the five-lobed flowers, pale white and blue-lavender forms, narrow foliage, and low annual habit. Reference photographs are not included in this repository.

Prompt:

> Original portrait 4:5 botanical hero artwork for a sophisticated dark California scientific consulting website. Nearly-black forest green background #101c17, soft blended edges, no paper. Specifically depict California sandblossoms, Linanthus parryae: a miniature annual with low-growing flower clusters, slender short green stems and fine narrow linear leaves. Fused shallow funnel corollas ending in exactly five broad rounded lobes, ivory white and pale periwinkle-lilac, tiny yellow throats/stamens and subtle dark purple marks near the throat. Delicate translucent petals and faint veins. Loose colony through center and right with natural angles and small buds; quiet negative space along left and perimeter. Refined almost-photographic botanical painting, moody soft side lighting, cream/lilac flowers and sage stems, natural dark depth. No glow, fantasy, broad foliage, cosmos, roses, six-petaled lilies, large yellow disks, text, chart, frame, logo, or watermark.

## Writing

Use direct service names, supported technical capabilities, and example deliverables. Avoid slogans, generic promises, invented client results, and employment endorsements. Public copy omits the founders' names and personal contact details. Descriptions of past expertise do not assign ownership of employer methods, models, data, or products to this company.
